## Canonical closure statement

The HYPE design is considered **maximally specified at the architectural/SOP level** when the combined documents cover:

- RELEASED, RADAR, RUMORS, WATCH, DISCOVERY, CORRELATOR;
- RTA, MOD, RTA-specific accessories, POD, AIO, BORO and clones;
- signal-observation 30d separate from release 30d and long-range forecasts;
- immutable evidence/event timeline;
- source lineage / independent-origin logic;
- product identity, novelty, region, batch, variant and clone separation;
- EC, IC, DC, LP, NC, HM, CC;
- direct official, social, reviewer, community, retail, distributor, clone, OEM/B2B, event, regulatory, IP, patent, certification, advertising, software/app, corporate, infrastructure, archive, newsletter, logistics, recall and media-intelligence families;
- multilingual/region-aware query generation;
- automatic new-source discovery and quarantine;
- source policy and technical-health lifecycle;
- explicit negative evidence/corrections;
- operational SOPs and QA;
- backtest/calibration and safe cutover;
- explicit lawful/public blind spots.

This does **not** mean every future URL, private group or unpublished prototype can be enumerated in advance. Maximum practical coverage is achieved by the combination of a broad curated registry and a self-expanding source graph.

## Change-control rule

Any future material addition/removal to HYPE truth semantics, source families, scoring or SOPs must:

1. update the relevant canonical document;
2. add/adjust a regression or acceptance test;
3. record the reason/version;
4. avoid weakening truth gates solely to increase result count.
